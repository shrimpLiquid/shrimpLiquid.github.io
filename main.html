Cut all the tomatoes in half and throw them on a sheet pan. Peel and slice the shallot (I like 1/4 circles) and put them on the sheet pan. Peel and coarsely chop the garlic (if it's super fine it's likely to burn), pick and chop the rosemary, seed and slice the chili if you're using it, and put all that on the sheet pan.
